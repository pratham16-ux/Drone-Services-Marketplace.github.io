Requirements

✔ FAA Part 107

Or your region's equivalent commercial drone certification.

✔ Liability Insurance

Minimum $1M coverage, verified before activation.

✔ Own Equipment

A registered aircraft suited to at least one service category.

✔ Portfolio Sample

Three prior flights or a short test mission for review.

Pilot FAQ

Common questions from applicants.

Is there a fee to join?+

No sign-up fee. Stackly takes a small service fee only from completed, paid missions.

Can I fly part-time?+

Yes — you choose which missions to accept and set your own availability.

What if I don't have Part 107 yet?+

You'll need commercial certification before activation, but you can start the application in the meantime.
