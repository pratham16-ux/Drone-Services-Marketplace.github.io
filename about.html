About Stackly

We're building the trust layer for commercial drone flight.

Stackly started with one question: why is it still hard to book a licensed drone pilot? Today we connect thousands of businesses with a vetted, insured pilot network across 58 countries.

Our mission

Make professional drone work as easy to book as a taxi.

Businesses were stuck choosing between expensive in-house drone teams and unverified freelancers. Stackly's marketplace verifies every pilot's license, insurance and equipment — so booking a flight takes minutes, not weeks of vetting.

Trust first

Every pilot is licensed and insured before their first mission.

Global reach

Local pilots in 58 countries, matched in hours.

Fair economics

Pilots set their rate; clients see transparent pricing.

Safety-led

Airspace and permit checks run before every flight.

Our story

From a two-person pilot directory to a global marketplace.

2021 — Founded

Stackly launched as a directory of certified pilots in three cities.

2022 — Marketplace launch

Booking, payments and airspace checks moved fully online.

2023 — International expansion

Stackly's pilot network crossed 25 countries.

2024 — Enterprise program

Recurring inspection and mapping contracts launched for large accounts.

2026 — 3,400+ pilots strong

Stackly now serves 58 countries with same-day matching.
